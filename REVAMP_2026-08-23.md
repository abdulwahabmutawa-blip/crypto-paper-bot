## The diagnosis (what actually failed, in evidence order)
1. Exits keyed to clocks sold correctly-identified moves early (stall clock:
   +1.6% avg giveback, +5.5% avg post-exit run; 24h cap captured +7.2%
   median of moves). Fixed 08-21 (fuel-gone) and 08-23 (climax, terminal
   dip, 48h cap, one-tier ratchet).
2. Late entries into spent moves (every entry >15% off the 24h low lost).
   Fixed 08-20 (15% cap). Watcher lane 0-for-9 real: bar raised 08-23.
3. Fee drag vs. edge: 3+1 all-in round trips/day = 15-24%/month of book if
   every slot fires — larger than any verified small-account edge. Gate now
   demands 4x the round trip, both halves of the record positive, and a
   payoff shape (avg win / avg loss >= 1 unless hit >= 65%).
4. No exposure cap outside the strategy: all-in x -6% x 4 = -24%/day worst
   case vs the verified durable profile of <=25% MDD per YEAR. Circuit
   breaker added.
5. Thin-book and venue-failure risk never guarded (Oct-10-2025 class).
   Depth gate + API-burst freeze added.
6. Regime blindness: ignition's record collapsed post-wave; revival fired
   4/4 in the crash. Regime gating by family added; the gate's both-halves
   test is the structural antidote to regime luck.
7. Calendar blindness: unlock cliffs (~90% negative within 30d). Veto added
   (defensive guard; the Oracle still records it as a scored lens).
